Download YouTube thumbnails in every size

Every YouTube video exposes its thumbnail at a handful of fixed sizes, from the tiny 120x90 preview up to the full HD 1280x720 image used on the watch page. Paste any video or Shorts link above and this tool shows the sizes that exist for that video, so you can preview and save exactly the resolution you need.

Handy for making reaction or comparison content, referencing a video in a blog post or presentation, recovering your own channel's artwork, or studying what makes a good thumbnail.

No server, no tracking

The thumbnail files come straight from YouTube's image servers to your browser; this page has no backend and stores nothing. Note that a video's thumbnail belongs to its creator: for anything beyond personal reference, get their permission. More free tools live on the Web Utility Tools hub.

Why is there no HD (1280x720) option for some videos?

YouTube only generates the maxresdefault image when the video was uploaded in HD. Older or low-resolution uploads stop at 640x480, and this tool simply hides sizes that do not exist.

Does it work for Shorts and live streams?

Yes. Shorts links (youtube.com/shorts/ID), youtu.be short links, embed links and plain video IDs all work; live streams get thumbnails once the stream has one.

Can I download a thumbnail in 4K?

1280x720 is the largest size YouTube publishes for normal videos. Sites advertising "4K thumbnails" upscale the same 1280x720 file; if you need it larger, upscale it in an image editor with the original aspect ratio.
